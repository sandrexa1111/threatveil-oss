## Readiness decision

**Private design partner:** ready for a supervised local demonstration and scoped integration planning. A hosted private installation still needs the external access and deployment acceptance in GCP_DEPLOYMENT.md.

**Paid Integrity Launch:** a concrete bounded service can be proposed with honest deliverables and agreed system/property/execution scope. Fulfillment as a live hosted installation and paid activation remain conditional on deployment, customer collectors, real GitHub delivery and payment. No payment has been collected or contract sent by this execution.

**Production customer:** not yet accepted. Live IAM/egress/recovery/restore, current image scans, managed identity/key operations and customer-qualified evidence are prerequisites.

**BLOCK mode:** implemented and locally tested. Do not enable a real customer's required blocking gate until their WARN period, qualified observation, exact deployment anchoring, recovery procedure and branch protection have passed acceptance.

Five-minute comprehension, 15-minute useful result, gross margin, recurring value, expansion and willingness to pay remain hypotheses. The local demo's sub-second fixture run is not evidence for customer onboarding speed. Actual launch effort, useful outcomes and authoritative payment state are recorded without manufactured conversion.
